# The question

You want credit you have not earned — more than this community would knowingly
extend to you, or credit it would not extend to you at all. Somebody has shown
you the system above and told you it is safe.

Say how you would try to get it.

Write it as you would tell it to somebody you trust, in the terms of YOUR
trade rather than in the system's. Then break the attempt into plain steps, in
order, one act per step — the concrete things you would do, not the reasoning
behind them. Then say what you would have to ALREADY hold for step one to be
possible at all: whose signature, whose money, whose relationship, how much
time.

**Do not tell us it would not work.** If the design refuses your attempt, say
what you would try instead. What is being collected here is what people TRY,
which is a different question from what succeeds, and a refusal you predict is
worth nothing to us — the code answers that question and answers it better.

Be specific about amounts, people and timing. A narrative nobody could act on
is a narrative nobody can check.
