\begin{abstract}
\noindent
\edet\ is a clearing medium for communities of mutual producers. Debt originates
peer-to-peer at the point of sale, discharges by circulating onward as its bearer delivers
value, and cannot be held: no token, no bearer instrument, no hoardable store of value.
A member accumulates dated claims on named debtors, and \emph{capacity} --- bounded,
non-transferable, and not a possession, since it lives in the stakes others have placed on
them.

Capacity is the \textbf{maximum flow into an account from the community's underwriters},
across directed stakes recording what creditors have placed on one another. Nobody can
stake beyond what they may confer, and outstanding credit reserves the flow that justified
it. The ledger keeps stakes and obligations and computes the cut, so there is no score to
award, adjust or revoke. An underwriter is any member who has declared a supply, an
accepted liability seated only by a ceremony: genesis, or an amendment the community
endorsed. The role is open, so a community is not limited to its founders; nor is it
enriched from inside, since a supply declared against capacity earned within the
community would reallocate credit without creating any --- what a community can carry is
its seed plus what later underwriters bring from outside. Admission, reputation and credit
limits are that one number, with no attestation, probationary tier, sponsorship, admission
or creation transition: an account is seated by the first bonded trade that names its key.

Sybil resistance follows from max-flow/min-cut rather than from a rule: the capacity of
any \emph{set} of accounts is bounded by the edges crossing into it. Minting identities
confers nothing, a colluding member passes on no more than they hold, fabricated volume
never crosses the boundary of the set that fabricated it and so is arithmetically zero
with nothing to detect, and attack costs exactly what participation costs.

Two structural commitments carry the rest. \emph{Capacity underwrites, it does not
permit}: credit beyond a debtor's capacity is allowed but uninsured, borne by the creditor
alone, which lets a community start from zero without a starter allowance for anyone who
can generate keys. \emph{Ledger-scoped total order}: a ledger is one replicated state
machine under Byzantine-fault-tolerant consensus among validators its own members operate,
with no world-global chain, no gas and no fee token. A ledger is not a community: a
community is a region of the stake graph, the boundary between two is the absence of a
path, one ledger carries as many as its members form, and there is no bridge.

The guarantees are invariants audited over the whole state after every block: the cut
bound on every account set, reservations conserved against outstanding credit, no stake
above what its creditor could confer, neither decay nor withdrawal able to age collateral
out from under a live debt, and loss mutualisation bounded by what each underwriter
declared.

Two boundaries remain, both economic, and acceptance is not one of them: a sale
discharges before it creates, so a member who owes is paid by ceasing to owe. What binds
is what makes clearing possible at all, a sufficient share of what a community buys being
available from within it --- a property of the community, ascertainable before it founds
anything. And zero is absorbing: a community that underwrites nothing at genesis can trade
on uninsured credit indefinitely, but can leave that state only through a founding
ceremony.
\end{abstract}
