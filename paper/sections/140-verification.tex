\section{Verification programme}
\label{sec:verification}

\subsection{Layers}

\paragraph{Proved.} The results of \S\ref{sec:security} are proved from
Definitions~\ref{def:stake}--\ref{def:reservation} and max-flow/min-cut, and they are
short: a bound re-argued per attack is a bound that will eventually be re-argued wrongly.

\paragraph{Enforced.} Seven invariants are audited over the whole state on the commit path
rather than in tests --- after every committed block on a validator, and after every
transition in the harnesses --- so a violation halts the chain rather than propagating;
what that costs per block is \S\ref{sec:implementation}:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item the \textbf{cut bound} (Invariant~\ref{inv:capacity}), evaluated over \emph{sets}
        and never only singletons --- all insured debtors together, and, per underwriter,
        everyone drawing through them. Two details are load-bearing: the cut is measured
        on a pristine residual, and only supply reaching the set from \emph{outside} it
        counts on the left, as the network construction drops the source arc of any
        underwriter inside the measured set; both are exercised by probes that fail when
        the clause they hold is removed, not only by fixtures in which the audit is
        expected to pass;
  \item \textbf{reservation conservation} (Invariant~\ref{inv:res-conservation}), exactly,
        in integer minor units;
  \item the \textbf{stake bound}. What an edge may carry is what its creditor could confer
        \emph{at the height it was written} --- at settlement, not at acceptance ---
        enforced where it is written rather than re-derived here, because a creditor's
        own backing legitimately moves afterwards and an audit flagging an edge above its
        creditor's present capacity would halt a healthy chain; the stored graph is held
        to every edge naming real accounts and carrying something;
  \item the \textbf{decay floor}: no edge sits below its live reservation;
  \item the \textbf{supply floor}: no underwriter's declaration sits below the flow
        committed through it;
  \item \textbf{determinism}: capacity recomputed from committed state reproduces the
        value the block was validated against, bit for bit;
  \item \textbf{seat conservation}: the seat reservation maps are exactly the sum of what
        the live seats hold, every seat is a flow from the source to its sponsor, and a
        sponsor is a member --- which the sweep keeps true by never retiring a row that
        sponsors a live seat. It claims conservation and nothing else: a seat is
        deliberately \emph{not} floored against decay or against the declaration it draws
        through (Property~\ref{prop:seat}), and a seat's \emph{size} is compared to
        nothing in either direction, because both bounds are properties of the write --- a
        seat takes one bond unit at the unit then in force, a re-denomination floors it,
        and an amendment of the unit leaves it where it is; a clause comparing a standing
        seat against the live unit halts every node on the first downward amendment of the
        one dial the design offers for the seat count.
\end{enumerate}

The same audit checks obligation conservation (Invariant~\ref{inv:conservation}) --- each
account's cached debt against a recount of the book, and the two totals against each
other, as exact integer equality --- and the structural facts no cut expresses: no contract
names one member on both sides, a validator is an active member, arbitration attestations
come only from the panel both parties pinned, and the key index round-trips in both
directions. Non-negativity needs no check: every stored amount is an unsigned integer, so
the type carries the floor.

\paragraph{Searched.} A population of behaving agents --- honest traders, a deadbeat, a
wash ring, a sybil farm, a griefer, a governance coalition, a panic exiter, an
honest-then-evil sleeper, a late defaulter and a hoarder --- runs over the same transition
function, and every transition it emits is judged by the same audit. The programme is
split three ways on purpose. \emph{Does the mechanism hold under adversarial play} is a
search, answered by deterministic strategies over the alphabet. \emph{Does it produce a
working economy under plausible behaviour} is distributional, answered by seeded
populations against a control that is the same seed with every treatment seat replaced
by an honest trader and aged the same number of epochs --- advancing an epoch decays every
stake, so a control that has not advanced the same number is not one. \emph{Would people
join, and what would they try} is generative, and the only part a language model touches:
it produces narratives, which are hypotheses, which the two halves above judge. No figure
a persona produced appears in this paper, because a generator whose behavioural variance
is two orders of magnitude below the phenomenon's would be describing itself.

Each run is one seed and replays bit-identically, so a violated invariant is reported
with the seed, the tick, the agent, the intent, the transaction and the command that
reproduces it. Twenty-four scenes are pinned as a gate; a strategy that bites is promoted by
hand into a named regression test rather than by replaying its seed, so the regression
does not depend on the strategy code that found it.

\paragraph{Measured.} Each claim has an adversarial test that fails if the property
regresses --- gates, not illustrations. Every row below names a probe in the tree: a
claim's \emph{measurement} must exist, not only its wording.

\subsection{What has been measured}

and a reference over \texttt{scipy}'s maximum flow written by people who had never heard
of this design --- and the fixtures demand bit-identical agreement rather than closeness.

the write one, which includes it. The reference states the seat bound too, and reaches the
same count from one edge of $500$ that the kernel does. The rows are grouped by the

\subsection{Negative results}

Five designs were measured and rejected; the reasons constrain any future revision.

\emph{Local approximation of trust does not localize.} On a graph with overlapping
community membership, a neighbourhood-truncated computation is accurate only once the
neighbourhood has grown to most of the graph: at two hops the capacity error is tens of
percent, at three the ball covers $67$--$100\%$ of the accounts. The ball is a constant
\emph{fraction} of the graph, not a constant size, so cost independent of network size is
unattainable by truncation; bounding the community, not the walk, is what works.

\emph{Flow cannot originate everywhere, and direction alone does not fix it.} With every
account an unbounded source, a colluder's honest $300$ compounds to $10{,}000{,}300$ in
three rounds, and directional stakes do not prevent it; supply must be bounded, which
Definition~\ref{def:underwriter} does.

\emph{Unbounded supply makes an underwriter an unbounded trust point.} With infinite
supply, one underwriter backing $21$ accounts confers $52{,}500$ rather than the $2{,}500$
they could confer on any one of them; bounding supply bounds the aggregate.

\emph{Settled volume is not a sound measure}: it is fabricable by two signatures
(Remark~\ref{rem:not-volume}).

\emph{Independent evaluation leaks by the number of evaluators}: $200\times$ with two
hundred creditors, which forces reservation and, with it, a serialization point
(Remark~\ref{rem:order}).

\subsection{Status}

The model is specified and built. The kernel computes capacity as an integer maximum
flow and is cross-pinned against an independent implementation; the state layer drives
the whole alphabet of twenty transitions and audits all seven invariants after every one
of them, on the commit path rather than in tests; the substrate runs --- consensus, block
production and the state root with its inclusion proofs in one node binary, custody and
signing in a wallet client. What has not happened is an external audit, and nothing here
claims production readiness: the model has not run a pilot genesis, on-device custody has
not been exercised end to end, and no party outside the project has reviewed any of it.

What a green gate claims is read narrowly, because the loose readings fail silently.
\textbf{A passing suite is a claim about what its tests assert}, and a test can assert a
defect --- intended behaviour is a description a defect can wear through a release.
\textbf{A passing invariant is not a claim about what it does not count}: all seven stay
green while a discharge destroys a creditor's recourse, because obligation is conserved
by that move and what dies is recourse, which no invariant counts ---
Property~\ref{prop:no-downgrade} has its own probe for exactly that reason. And
\textbf{a green build is a claim about the gates that exist}: no gate in this tree reads
a document.

\subsection{Method}

Four rules produce most of what the sections above assert; a fifth --- that a claim's
measurement must exist and name its probe --- is what the tables above are.

\paragraph{Test at the quantifier the theorem uses.} The characteristic defect of this
class of model is a bound asserted over \emph{sets} and tested over \emph{singletons}:
one underwriter's supply drawn by several debtors, a reservation held along several
hops, a supply withdrawn while several obligations stand on it, a write channel measured
on one member of a chain. A per-account check passes in every one, and in the last it
leaves a whole theorem vacuous --- the quantity is a growth rate, and a single point on it
looks like a constant.

\paragraph{Measure against the counterfactual, not against before-and-after.} Standing
decays on its own, so walking thirty-one epochs to reach a default and then comparing
the creditor's capacity with what it was credits the default with the decay. The
uninsured-loss probe is therefore two identical communities, the same epochs walked in
each, and the only difference the thing under test.

\paragraph{A premise worth measuring may turn out to be structural.} The cut memo rests
on ``most blocks decrease nothing'', which reads as a hope about workloads. It is not
empirical: Definition~\ref{def:stake} is a peak rule and settlement is the graph's only
writer, so no ordinary traffic can lower an edge, and the complete list of what can is
three transitions. Checking the sentence turns a hope about workloads into a property of
the model --- the paragraph above, run in the other direction.

\paragraph{When the code and the document disagree, find out which one is coherent.}
Neither is the specification by default. The arbitral award runs from creditor to debtor
because it is the buyer's remedy for non-delivery, which a document can state backwards
in perfect confidence; the underwriter who cannot leave is the throttle working, which a
document can as confidently call a defect. A comment or a paragraph is the one part of a
tree no gate reads, which is where a superseded model survives longest.
